Relevant to **AI and Cognition**, embedding artificial intelligence (AI) tools raises vital questions about how judgment, critical thinking and intellectual independence may change. Large Language Models (LLMs) interpolate, and do not extrapolate. Interpolation is about calculating probabilities, not envisioning possibilities. We think in terms of probabilities when we are afraid. Creating online spaces supporting meaningful and healthy social interaction and engagement between people for exercising judgement, critical thinking and intellectual independence becomes more important and valuable to individuals, groups and society.  

The Web3 industry seeks mass adoption for blockchain technologies. However, cryptocurrency trading practices tend to reflect zero-sum thinking focussing only on addressing the most fundamental requirements for human survival. Currently, blockchain development efforts tend to focus on adoption of private blockchain technologies by centralized institutions and authorities leveraging only better time to finality and 24/7 availability in order to increase market share and maximize profits. As a consequence, the unique benefits that public blockchain technologies may offer to the commons and society as a whole are not realized or recognized. A successful public blockchain requires decentralization, becoming worthless when a single actor controls more than 50 percent of the network. Successful mass adoption of blockchain-based applications must involve education and practice using blockchain technologies over the longer term to help find and experience the benefits of working to improve balance and trust in relationships with others through peaceful cooperation, collaboration and compromise.

Relevant to **AI and Relationships**, FamilyDAO offers a socially useful, positive-sum and easy-to-use product for the lay person to begin understanding, learning and experiencing first hand the exciting new benefits, possibilities and advantages that the discovery of blockchain technologies offers the world.

We all have families, or families of origin. We live in a time where more than ever family members may be separated—by geography, emotions, politics, natural disasters or war. FamilyDAO allows you to create a dedicated, distributed and secure blockchain just for you and your family, stored only on your physical or virtual computers and devices.

On your family blockchain, you can create a genogram family tree including relationships spanning any number of generations.<sup><a href="#orgchart">1</a></sup> Only you and family members you designate in your FamilyDAO can add or update records in your family tree to preserve, commemorate and share details related to births, deaths, graduations, marriages, divorces, anniversaries and other significant events.<sup><a href="#normalizing">2</a></sup> Records may also associate additional text, images, audio or video recordings, for example to save traditional recipes for the benefit of family as well as a wider community. Family members may create wills without the need of a notary public. You may designate trusted or professional associates to help administer your FamilyDAO. Together, you can add important supporting documents and records of any nature (legal or medical, for example); chat or broadcast personal news or information shared only with family and designated friends; and, vote privately to make decisions on important family matters including administration of the FamilyDAO collectively. You can define rules for weighting and voting roles within your family, as well as rules for creating and assigning rules for weighting and voting roles, as you may choose. Zero-knowledge proofs may be useful for verifying voting results while respecting privacy.

FamilyDAO offers functionality to support and administer income sharing, helping to facilitate transactions related to informal borrowing from relatives and friends that may cover daily needs, housing, school fees or unexpected costs for example.

If family members are far away, then maintaining communication by distributing and synchronizing the nodes storing your family blockchain around the world in real time using the Internet becomes an advantage by helping to increase decentralization, securing and preserving your family relationships and history as long as you and your family may want. Nodes that disconnect from the network automatically synchronize when reconnected. FamilyDAO blockchains are designed to be passed down, inherited and grown over generations.

FamilyDAO may help provide new opportunities for families learning how to resolve or overcome internal conflict, offering a method for family members to establish healthy boundaries while staying connected to continue cooperating on important family matters using blockchain as a trusted intermediary tool.

FamilyDAO offers your family the capacity to:

* Preserve identity and knowledge
* Organize collective affairs
* Manage collectively held or generational resources, and
* Negotiate relationships with external institutions

FamilyDAO is **not** intended as the only medium that families may use to communicate and interact.

Using FamilyDAO builds an immutable, unique and timeless resource and reference for current and future generations of your family, curated only by and for the people who matter most<!--, without interference by centralized authorities, institutions and organizations that may seek to invade your privacy to profit from capturing, processing and using your personal data-->.

The current proposal aims to elicit requirements for FamilyDAO features and functionality from a multicultural sample of individuals and family offices; prepare additional product and project management planning documents; and, network to assemble resources capable of developing and implementing FamilyDAO sustainably as an open-source, decentralized software application development project that facilitates studying, changing and improving the design transparently.

# Significance and Expected Outcomes

FamilyDAO aims to offer a secure, private and easy-to-use platform helping to honour generations of families, human connections and social networks without centralized data storage on servers controlled by technology companies, for example to train artificial intelligence tools.

The expected outcome is FamilyDAO organically becoming a sustainable, mainstream and everyday tool—not an extractive algorithm—for the general public.
